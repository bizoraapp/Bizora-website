Licensing help

Everything around activating and renewing your Bizora license.

How do I activate my license?

After payment you'll receive an activation key. In Bizora, go to Settings → License → Activate License and enter the key.

Where do I enter my activation key?

Under Settings → License → Activate License inside the Bizora app.

What happens when my license expires?

A few days before your license expires, Bizora shows a reminder with an activation button. Tapping it takes you to a secure Selar checkout, and once payment is complete, Selar automatically redirects you to WhatsApp.

I need help with activation. What should I do?

Contact us using any of the support options on this page and we'll help you through it.

Working offline, and protecting your data

Once installed, Bizora supports your core business operations offline. Internet is needed for installation, updates, contacting support, and online payment or licensing. Your business data is stored on your own device — so backing up regularly is what keeps your records protected. If you'd like help understanding backup and restore, just get in touch.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bizora work without internet?

Yes. Bizora is designed to support core business operations offline once installed.

How do I get started?

Start the free trial, install Bizora, and follow the setup process. On iPhone, open Bizora in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

How do I contact Bizora?

Use WhatsApp, email or phone through the support options on this page.

I need help activating my license. What should I do?

Contact Bizora support and we'll assist you with activation.

Where is my business data stored?

Bizora is offline-first, and your business data is stored locally on your own device.

Can Bizora be used on multiple devices at the same time?

Bizora is currently designed for one device at a time.

What should I include when contacting support?

Explain the problem clearly, mention the feature you were using, and include a screenshot if it helps show what happened.

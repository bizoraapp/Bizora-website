What is a point of sale (POS) app?

The point of sale is the place where a sale happens: the counter in your shop. A POS app records the sale, works out the total, takes the payment and gives your customer a receipt. Bizora does this on your phone or computer, and the same sale updates your stock, your customer accounts and your reports.

Point of sale (POS) questions

What is a point of sale (POS) app?

A point of sale (POS) app is the tool you use at the counter to make a sale. It records what was sold and the total, takes the payment, and gives the customer a receipt. Bizora also keeps your stock, customer debts and reports up to date from the same sales.

Can I use Bizora's POS without internet?

Yes. Once Bizora is installed, your core business work, including sales, inventory, and credit and debt tracking, keeps running with no internet connection. You only need to connect for things like installing the app, getting updates and contacting support.

How do I give my customer a receipt?

After a sale, Bizora generates a receipt with your business name and logo. You can print it on a thermal receipt printer, send it straight to WhatsApp, or download it as a PDF to share in any messaging app.

Can I sell on credit with Bizora?

Yes. Bizora records both cash sales and credit sales. A credit sale is tied to a customer account, so you can see outstanding balances and track repayments as customers pay you back.

Do I need a special device or a receipt printer?

Bizora works on an Android phone or a computer. A thermal receipt printer is optional: you can also send receipts on WhatsApp or save them as a PDF.

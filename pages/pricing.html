How licensing works

Four straightforward steps from trial to a fully licensed Bizora.

1

Start your trial

Install Bizora and begin using the application. On iPhone, use Safari and choose Add to Home Screen from the Share menu.

2

Choose your plan

Select the licensing period that suits your business.

3

Complete payment

Follow the payment process we confirm with you directly.

4

Activate Bizora

Enter the activation key you receive, under Settings → License.

Renewing later is even simpler. A few days before your license expires, Bizora shows a reminder with an activation button right in the app. Tapping it takes you straight to a secure Selar checkout — no need to message us again — and once payment is complete, Selar automatically redirects you to WhatsApp.

Pricing questions

Can I try Bizora before paying?

Yes. Bizora provides a free trial so you can explore the product before purchasing a license.

Does Bizora work without internet?

Yes. Once installed, Bizora works offline for its core business operations. Internet is required for installation, updates, support and online payment.

Where is my business data stored?

Your business data is stored on your own device.

Can I use Bizora on multiple devices at the same time?

Bizora is currently designed for one device at a time.

How do I activate my license?

Go to Settings → License → Activate License, and enter the activation key you received after payment.

What happens when my license is close to expiring?

A few days before your license expires, Bizora shows a reminder with an activation button. Tapping it takes you to a secure Selar checkout, and once payment is complete, Selar automatically redirects you to WhatsApp.

Installed it? Here's what to do first

About ten minutes of setup, and you're ready to serve your first customer.

  1. Add your business name and upload your logo — they'll appear on every receipt and report you produce.
  2. Add your phone number and business details, so customers can reach you from the receipt.
  3. Add your products, with the cost price, selling price and how much stock you have on hand.
  4. Start selling. Ring up a sale, and hand your customer a receipt — printed on a thermal printer, sent on WhatsApp, or saved as a PDF.

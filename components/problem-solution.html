The problems Bizora addresses

These are the practical, everyday challenges the product is built around.

The notebook is doing more work than it can carry.

None of this is because you're bad at business — a notebook and memory can only carry so much.

The problem

Ten, twenty, thirty people owe you from credit. Some you remember. Some you quietly write off.

✓ Bizora fixes this

Every debt on record, with a name — how much, since when. When you follow up, you have the facts, not a guess.

The problem

It's 4pm, the shop is full, and some sales never make it into the book — there isn't time.

✓ Bizora fixes this

Type what was sold and for how much. Bizora keeps the record and does the arithmetic — no evening spent adding up a notebook by hand.

The problem

You promise a customer their item is coming. It finished three days ago and nobody told you.

✓ Bizora fixes this

Bizora counts down your stock as you sell and warns you before it runs out — while there's still time to reorder.

The problem

Customers ask for a receipt and you're writing one by hand — or telling them you don't give any.

✓ Bizora fixes this

Ring up the sale and issue a proper receipt on the spot — print it to a thermal printer, send it straight to WhatsApp, or save it as a PDF to share however your customer prefers.

The problem

By evening you're holding a stack of paper and a feeling that the numbers don't quite add up.

✓ Bizora fixes this

Your real profit, every single day — what came in, what went out, what you actually kept. One screen, in plain figures.

Does Bizora really work without internet?

Yes. Sales, inventory, and debt tracking all work offline — no data bundle needed, and nothing is uploaded while you trade.

Is my business data safe?

Nowhere but your own device, and no one but you. Your data belongs to your business — it's never sold or shared.

Can my staff use Bizora too?

Yes — Bizora supports staff accounts with permission controls for what each person can see and do.

What happens after my free trial ends?

You'll be prompted to license Bizora inside the app — start free, and pay only once it's already earned it back for you. Your data carries over, nothing is lost.

What devices does Bizora work on?

Bizora installs straight from your browser on Android phones, including low-end and budget devices. On iPhone, open Bizora in Safari, tap Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

What if my phone or computer is lost or damaged?

Use the backup option in Settings to export a backup file, then save it somewhere safe — a cloud drive or USB drive works well. If your device is ever lost, you restore from that file on your next one.
